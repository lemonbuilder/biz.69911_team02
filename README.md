## What we are building

An offline community service that sells real face-to-face conversation.

- **Small meetups.** People open a small meetup or join an open seat, so that everyone at the table gets to talk. The group size is still being tested.
- **AI stays off the table.** AI handles scheduling, screening applications, matching participants, and preparing a first topic. The conversation itself is left to people.
- **Verified conversation partners.** After each session, participants rate it. People who often join well-rated sessions become "verified conversation partners", and meetups with them cost extra.

## Who it is for

Adults who feel socially isolated: people who spend much of the day talking to messengers, video calls and generative AI, but have fewer and fewer people to talk to in depth.
